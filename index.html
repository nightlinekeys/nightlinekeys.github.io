Questions producers ask

What are the most useful Ableton Live 13 shortcuts to learn first?

Start with these: Tab switches between Session and Arrangement View, Space plays and stops, Cmd+D (Ctrl+D on Windows) duplicates, Cmd+E (Ctrl+E) splits a clip at the selection, Cmd+J (Ctrl+J) consolidates, Cmd+U (Ctrl+U) quantizes, Cmd+L (Ctrl+L) loops the selection, B toggles Draw Mode, C arms the selected tracks, F9 records into the Arrangement and O toggles the metronome. The full Starter 20 is listed above.

How are Ableton shortcuts different on Mac and Windows?

Most shortcuts swap Cmd for Ctrl and Option for Alt. A few change keys completely: Redo is Shift+Cmd+Z on Mac and Ctrl+Y on Windows, and full screen is Ctrl+Cmd+F on Mac and F11 on Windows. Each row in the tables above shows the Mac and Windows keys side by side.

What keyboard shortcuts are new in Ableton Live 13?

This page tags 38 shortcuts as new in Live 13, including Capture on N, creating or converting a Step Sequencer clip, changing the Step Sequencer size, and new device chain keys such as showing Device Slots and moving or renaming devices. They are taken from the Live 13 public beta and may change before the final release.

Why does a letter key play a note instead of running a shortcut?

When the computer MIDI keyboard is on, Live uses the letter rows to play notes. Press M to turn it off and the single-key shortcuts work again.

Do function keys like F9 work on a MacBook?

On most MacBooks the top row controls brightness and volume by default, so hold Fn with the function key, or change the setting in macOS Keyboard settings.

Is there a printable Ableton Live 13 shortcut cheat sheet?

Yes. A free Starter 20 PDF for Mac and Windows is at https://starseed73.gumroad.com/l/ableton-live-13-shortcuts. The full Night Line pack adds a print pack of the full list, an offline interactive map and wallpapers.

Do Ableton Live 12 shortcuts still work in Live 13?

Most core shortcuts carry over, such as play, record, duplicate, split, consolidate, quantize and loop. Live 13 adds new ones, which are tagged on this page.
